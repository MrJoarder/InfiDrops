Clear answers.

Which marketplaces does InfiDrops support?+

We support Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Etsy and Temu, including account setup and ongoing marketplace management. The exact approach depends on your business model and goals.

How do we begin working together?+

Start with a discovery call. We learn about your current operation, objectives and constraints, then recommend the most practical next step.

Can you work with new sellers?+

Yes. We can support launch-stage sellers as well as established businesses that need more structure, channel expertise or operational support.

Do you offer a custom plan?+

Yes. We shape every engagement around the marketplace channels, priorities and level of support that fit your business.

How much do your services cost?+

Pricing depends on the channels, scope and level of hands-on support you need. Book a strategy call and we'll recommend a fit from our engagement models before any commitment.

How soon can we expect results?+

Early wins like catalog and account health fixes often show up in the first 30 days, while compounding growth from strategy and optimization typically builds over 90 days and beyond.
